## About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist** with a postgraduate specialization in Data Science and a strong quantitative foundation from my **BSc & MSc in Physics** at UFJF (Universidade Federal de Juiz de Fora).

My physics background gave me a deep intuition for modeling complex systems, statistical reasoning, and finding signal in noisy data — skills that translate directly into building robust, end-to-end machine learning pipelines. I've built a portfolio of projects that goes from raw data all the way to deployed applications, with a consistent emphasis on **the right metric for the problem** and **explainability over black boxes**.

What I focus on:

- 🔍 **Explainable AI** — making models interpretable with SHAP and related techniques
- ⚙️ **End-to-end ML pipelines** — from raw data to deployed applications
- 📊 **Statistical rigor** — proper hypothesis testing, validation strategies, and metric selection
- ⚖️ **Imbalanced problems** — resampling strategies and PR-AUC-driven evaluation
- 🚀 **Deployment** — shipping models as Streamlit apps and FastAPI services

📄 *Currently leveling up:* advanced **SQL**, **cloud / data engineering** (Docker · FastAPI · AWS/GCP), and **MLOps**.
