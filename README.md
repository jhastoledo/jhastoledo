## About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist** with a postgraduate specialization in Data Science and a strong quantitative foundation from my **BSc & MSc in Physics** at UFJF (Universidade Federal de Juiz de Fora).

My background in physics gave me a deep intuition for modeling complex systems, statistical reasoning, and finding signal in noisy data — skills that translate directly into building robust machine learning pipelines.

I'm particularly interested in:

- 🔍 **Explainable AI** — making models interpretable with SHAP and related techniques
- ⚙️ **End-to-end ML pipelines** — from raw data to deployed applications
- 📊 **Statistical rigor** — applying proper hypothesis testing and validation strategies
- 🚀 **Deployment** — shipping models as Streamlit apps and FastAPI services
